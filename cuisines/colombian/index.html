HOW HOMETASTE WORKS

Context from lived experience, open to every diner.

Anyone can use this guide. People who grew up with, learned at home, or lived with Colombian food can add concrete notes about what feels familiar. These voices add cultural context without declaring a single version of the cuisine the only correct one.
